Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how First Gen Studios ("we," "us," or "our"), a DBA of Helping Hands Senior Errand Service LLC, handles information in our mobile games, including Galaxy Strike (the "Games").

Information we collect

Our Games do not collect, store, or share any personal information. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data, and you do not need an account to play.

Information stored on your device

Galaxy Strike saves your game settings and high scores only on your own device so they are there the next time you play. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising, analytics, and third parties

Our Games currently contain no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no in-app purchases, and they do not share data with third parties. If this ever changes, we will update this policy and the app's Google Play listing before the change takes effect.

Children's privacy

Because our Games do not collect personal information from anyone, they do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data security

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data held by us that could be exposed.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
