Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how First Gen Studios ("we," "us," or "our"), a DBA of Helping Hands Senior Errand Service LLC, handles information in our mobile games Galaxy Strike, Spades and Latchkey, and any other game we publish that links to this page (the "Games").

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or photos, and you do not need an account to play. The Games have no chat or social features.

Information stored on your device

Game progress and settings are saved only on your own device. This information is not sent to us. Uninstalling a Game, or clearing its data in your Android settings, permanently deletes it. We do not keep a copy.

Advertising

Some of our Games show ads. Those Games are marked "Contains ads" on their Google Play page. Ads are provided by Unity. When an ad loads, Unity collects device information such as your IP address and device identifiers, and information about the ads you see and tap. Unity handles this information under its own Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, which also explains how to request access to or deletion of that data.

You can reset your advertising ID or limit ad personalization in your Android device settings. If you buy "Remove Ads" in a Game, it stops showing ads between games; optional reward ads that you choose to watch remain available.

In-app purchases

Some of our Games offer optional in-app purchases, marked "In-app purchases" on their Google Play page. Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your card number or payment details. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles payment information.

How we use and share information

Game progress is used only to run the Games. We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers named above (Unity for ads, Google for purchases), or when required by law.

Data security

We do not collect or hold personal data on our own servers. Game progress stays on your device, and Unity and Google transmit their data over encrypted connections under their own security programs.

Children's privacy

Our Games are made for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
